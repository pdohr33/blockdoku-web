A free block puzzle you can play in your browser. Fit pieces onto the 9x9 board, clear complete rows, columns, and 3x3 boxes, and leave room for your next tray.

Choose a light or dark theme and switch sound on or off. Your scores stay in this browser.

How to play

  1. Place a piece from the tray into empty spaces on the 9x9 board.
  2. Fill a row, column, or 3x3 box to clear it and earn points.
  3. Place all three pieces to receive the next tray.
  4. Keep space available. The round ends when no remaining piece fits.

A few useful details

How do I play BlockDoku online?

Drag pieces from the tray onto the 9x9 board. Fill a complete row, column, or 3x3 box to clear it and score points. Place all three pieces to get a new tray. The round ends when none of the remaining pieces fits.

Which modes can I play here?

Classic is an untimed high-score game. Daily Challenge uses a board and piece sequence based on the date, so you can retry the same daily challenge and improve your score.

Where is my progress saved?

Scores and saved games stay in this browser. Clearing site data can remove them. Progress does not sync with the Android app.

Can I change the theme or sound?

Yes. Use the theme and sound controls below the board to choose a light or dark theme and turn game sounds on or off.
